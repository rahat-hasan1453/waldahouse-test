# Waldhaus am See, St. Moritz: Website & Business Research Audit

> **Project phase:** Research only. No design or code has been approved.
> **Rule:** Do not design a single pixel or write UI code without explicit go-ahead from Asgar (product/design owner).
> **Current site:** https://www.waldhaus-am-see.ch/en/
> **Audit date:** September 2026. Based on page markup and public listings, not a live browser session.

---

## TL;DR

- **Business:** Family-run 3★ Superior hotel, 50 rooms, directly on Lake St. Moritz. It was built in 1885 and has been owned by the Bernasconi family since 1983. Rooms cost about CHF 130–815. Its USPs are "only hotel directly on the lake", 5 minutes' walk from the station, the Devil's Place whisky bar (2,500+ whiskies) and a large wine cellar.
- **Reputation:** Location is near-perfect (Booking.com 9.7/10). Weak spots are value (Tripadvisor 3.9/5), reception service and dated interiors.
- **Website:** WordPress + Elementor (Hoteller theme). Room booking goes off-site to Mews, vouchers to e-guma, and the whisky shop to worldofwhisky.ch. Table booking is an email form labelled in German. There is no events flow at all.
- **Core redesign problem:** Trust, accuracy and routing four audiences (stay / dine & drink / celebrate & meet / gift & shop), more than aesthetics.

## 1. Business Overview

**Identity**
- Hotel & Restaurant Waldhaus am See, Via Dim Lej 6, 7500 St. Moritz. Tel +41 81 836 60 00.
- 3★ Superior, 50 rooms, about 100 beds, open 365 days a year, 35 year-round staff.
- Self-described: "Only hotel directly on the lake, 5 minutes walk from the station, unique view towards Maloja, quiet location."

**History**
- 1885: built as "Waldschlössli".
- 1983: Claudio Bernasconi bought and renamed it.
- 2011: son Sandro Bernasconi joined. Sole director since Nov 2014. Thomas Stieger became co-host in 2025.
- Investments: CHF 9M (1995), CHF 2.2M (2013), CHF 3M (2019: spa, 25 rooms, larger restaurant window front).
- 1995: Devil's Place whisky bar founded with 400 whiskies from Bernasconi's personal collection.

**Positioning:** Traditional mid-market "affordable St. Moritz with a view", plus connoisseur experiences (whisky, cigars, wine) that competitors can't copy. It is a member of the Top 3 Star Hotels of Switzerland group, tagged Family, Pure nature, Events & Celebrations, Wellness and By the water.

**Target audiences (inferred)**
- Couples and romantic short breaks
- Rail tourists (Glacier / Bernina Express, one-night stops)
- Whisky, cigar and wine enthusiasts, including non-staying bar guests
- Families (two-floor family rooms, apartments, package weeks)
- Dog owners (pets CHF 10/night)
- Locals and day visitors (terrace, lounge, restaurant, NYE dinner)
- Small events: weddings, banquets, birthdays, seminars
- Active guests (hiking and biking packages, coaching, free fishing boat)

**USPs**
- Lakeside location with panoramic views
- Devil's Place: 2,500+ whiskies, cigar lounge with walk-in humidor, own bottlings
- Wine cellar "Cave Waldhaus". Figures conflict across sources: 1,000+ / 1,300 positions and 25,000 / 30,000 bottles.
- Free station shuttle
- Spa free for hotel guests
- Open year-round

**Awards: verification status (important for copy)**

| Claim | Reality | Action |
|---|---|---|
| "Multiple-Time Winner of Switzerland's Best 3-Star Hotel" | Bilanz winner 1996, 1997, 1999. Recent Karl Wild ranks: roughly 2nd–7th (5th in 2024/25). Not in the 2025/26 top 3. | Reframe with dates; don't imply it's current |
| "Guinness World Record since 1999" | Guinness's current page lists The Whiskey House (USA) as holder since 2019 (2,467 whiskies). Forbes (Jan 2026) still credits Devil's Place. | **Verify with the hotel before reusing** |
| Wine Spectator | Grand Award 1994. Best of Award of Excellence listed in 2018. Current status unverified. | Verify |
| Leading Bars of the World | Member (cited by the hotel and third parties) | OK |

**Price level:** CHF 130 (single) to CHF 815 (suite). Budget-to-mid for St. Moritz, but not cheap in absolute terms, which explains the weak "Value" score.

## 5. UX/UI Observations

### Strengths
- Persistent phone number and "Reservation" button in the header.
- USPs (whisky, wine, lake view, terrace) are surfaced on the homepage.
- Opening hours are prominent, which helps day guests.
- Real, recent photography (2025).
- Menus are published.
- "From" prices shown up front.

### Defects (prioritised)
1. **🚨 Hidden spam links** in every page footer: tinysexdolls.com, replicawatches.to, bellswigs.com, watchesreplica.is, agwigs.com. The theme is compromised or injected. This is a security, SEO and reputation risk. **Fix before anything else.**
2. **Broken or misrouted links:**
   - The brochure PDF URL is broken (concatenated path).
   - The "Book Your Stay Now" slide links to the restaurant.
   - Some English links go to German pages.
   - Award logos link to `#`.
   - The partner logo points to the old brand, best3starhotels.ch.
3. **Language mixing:** German form labels, buttons ("Senden", "anmelden", "Reservierung"), footer copy and dropdowns on English pages. Inconsistent spellings ("Whiskey" vs "Whisky", "Rennovation").
4. **Contradictory or outdated content:**
   - The NYE 2025 special is still on the homepage in Sep 2026.
   - COVID-era spa text ("max 6").
   - "April 2025" hours.
   - Half-board is described as both 4-course and 5-course.
   - Beds given as 100 vs 101.
   - Single-room price CHF 130 vs 100/125.
   - Comfort lake-view is cheaper than Comfort non-lake.
5. **Template leftovers:** the placeholder label "Sport" appears on every slide, and slider markup is duplicated.
6. **Accessibility:**
   - `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
   - Alt text is camera filenames ("IMG_3532", "DJI_0018").
   - The price list is an image only.
   - The "Hotel" menu parent is `#`.
   - Image CAPTCHA.
   - Contrast and focus are unmeasured and need a manual check.
7. **Hierarchy:** no date picker or availability entry point on the homepage, no "why stay here", and no rooms, offers or reviews on the homepage.
8. **Trust signals:** no review scores shown, and award claims are vague or dated.
9. **Performance (unmeasured):** about 20+ full-size images on the homepage, duplicated markup, Elementor and Google Fonts. Likely heavy on mobile; run Lighthouse.

## 6. Technical Notes

- **CMS:** WordPress + Elementor (4.2–4.3). Child theme `hoteller-child` (ThemeForest "Hoteller" by ThemeGoods; its built-in booking system is unused). Multilingual support probably via Polylang or similar.
- **Booking engine:** Mews Booking Engine (`app.mews.com/distributor/{id}`), probably also the property management system (PMS, the hotel's back-office reservation system).
  - Supports an embeddable widget and deep links (`setStartDate`, `showRooms`, `showRates(roomId)`).
  - A fully custom booking UI via the Mews Booking Engine API is **Enterprise-only**.
- **Integrations:** e-guma (vouchers), Google Maps and Street View, worldofwhisky.ch (separate e-commerce), PDF menus in wp-content.
- **Social:** Facebook, LinkedIn (footer). Contact page also shows Tripadvisor, YouTube, Instagram. Instagram @hotel_waldhaus_am_see has about 25K followers.
- **Unknown (verify in browser dev tools):** analytics / tag manager, cookie consent tool, newsletter provider. No review widget.

## 7. Reputation (captured Sep 2026)

| Platform | Score | Reviews |
|---|---|---|
| Tripadvisor | 4.3/5, #17 of 32 in St. Moritz (Location 4.8, Rooms 4.1, **Value 3.9**, Cleanliness 4.4, Service 4.2, Sleep 4.4) | 576 |
| Booking.com | Location 9.7/10 | 1,607 |
| Expedia | 9.2/10 | 553 |
| KAYAK (aggregate) | 8.8/10 | 2,381 |
| HolidayCheck | 5.5/6, 100% recommend | 46 |

**Praise:** location on the lake and near the station, views, whisky bar, Châteaubriand and fondue chinoise, farmer's buffet, comfortable beds, new spa, long-serving friendly staff.

**Complaints (2025–26):**
- Cold reception at check-in and check-out
- Slow restaurant service
- Self-service coffee machine at breakfast
- Shuttle not communicated / ends at 16:45
- Steep final climb with luggage
- Dated corridors and carpets, musty feel
- Small attic rooms
- Paid parking
- Weak dietary handling (gluten-free)
- Windowless spa that needs booking
- A "time warp" feel

**Implication for the redesign:** Lead with what is truly exceptional. Set honest expectations about room size, style, spa slots and parking. Use the site to communicate what reception doesn't: shuttle times, arrival route, breakfast window tables, dietary requests, spa booking. Many complaints are communication gaps the site can close.

## 8. Competitive Context (Upper Engadin)

- **Hotel Laudinella (4★, St. Moritz-Bad):** Same WordPress + Elementor stack, done well.
  - Product-based navigation (Rooms, Suites, Apartments, Gastronomy, Spa, Culture & Events, Activities).
  - Every room card deep-links into an on-brand booking engine (wbe.hotel-laudinella.ch).
  - Persistent category + dates search bar, dated promotions, award badges.
- **Waldhaus Sils (5★, Sils Maria):**
  - A contact panel separating room, restaurant and concierge.
  - Booking engine on its own subdomain.
  - "Shop & Vouchers", "Families" and "Meetings & celebrations" as main navigation items.
  - DE/EN/FR, a livecam, and a "Getting here" page.
- **Hauser Hotel (3★, village centre):** Closest direct peer, heavily OTA-driven (online travel agencies such as Booking.com). **Lesson:** direct-channel perks and bundles differentiate interchangeable 3★ hotels.
- **Top 3 Star Hotels partner page:** Currently sends Waldhaus traffic to an obsolete booking engine and URLs. Quick win.

**Best practices to adopt:**
- Booking entry points aware of room and dates
- On-brand or embedded booking engine
- Audience-based navigation
- Events page with capacities and an inquiry form
- Vouchers and shop in main navigation
- Separate room and restaurant contact lines
- An arrival page
- Three languages (DE/EN/FR)

## Key Opportunities (problems to solve, not solutions)

1. **Trust & hygiene (critical):** spam links, broken links, half-translated pages, contradictory prices and hours, expired specials.
2. **Booking conversion leakage:** off-domain Mews, lost room pre-selection, no date entry point, partner site on an obsolete engine.
3. **IA doesn't reflect the four audiences:** Stay / Dine & Drink / Celebrate & Meet / Gift & Shop. Events have no flow; vouchers and shop are disconnected.
4. **Table reservation is an email form, not a booking.** It is also misused for package inquiries.
5. **The unique story is under-used and partly outdated:** the Guinness and "multiple-time winner" claims need verification. Current proof (review scores, dated awards) is missing.
6. **Pre-arrival information gaps drive bad reviews:** shuttle, arrival route, spa slots, window tables, parking, dietary needs.
7. **Accessibility & mobile basics:** zoom blocked, filename alt text, image-only prices, performance unmeasured.
8. **Content governance:** duplicate slugs, legacy URLs indexed, inconsistent facts. Needs a single source of truth for prices, hours and capacities, plus a seasonal update workflow.
